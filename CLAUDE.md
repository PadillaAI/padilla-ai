# Padilla AI — Business Context

## The Goal
**$100,000/month revenue by November 2028.**
Every piece of work in this repo should ladder up to that. When helping, always think: does this move revenue, conversion, or client capacity?

## What the business is
Padilla AI is an AI agency selling automation to local/small businesses.

- **ARIA** — Automated Receptionist & Intelligence Assistant. The core product: answers calls/messages for businesses that miss them. Sold as a recurring service.
- **Missed Call Calculator** (`calculator.html`) — lead magnet that shows a business how much revenue missed calls cost them.
- **Lead/payment plumbing** — Netlify functions: `capture-lead.js`, `stripe-webhook.js`, `calendly-webhook.js`.

## The math to $100k/mo (reference)
Recurring-revenue paths, roughly:
- 200 clients × $500/mo, or
- 100 clients × $1,000/mo, or
- fewer clients + setup fees ($1.5k–$5k one-time) stacked on retainers.

Prioritize: raising price per client and recurring revenue over one-off builds.
