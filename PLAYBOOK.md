# The Padilla AI Playbook — $0 → $100k/mo by Nov 2028

Written July 2026. 28 months on the clock. Current state: 0 clients, $0 MRR.
This is the script. Don't reinvent it every morning — just execute today's line.

---

## THE NON-NEGOTIABLES (daily, Mon–Fri, no exceptions)

1. **10 real outreach touches before anything else.** A touch = a phone call, a walk-in, or a personalized DM/email to a specific local business owner. Mass blasts don't count. Do this BEFORE touching code, design, or "improving the site." Prospecting first, polish never before.
2. **Every lead gets a follow-up within 24 hours.** Most sales happen on follow-up 3–7. A lead that hasn't said "no" is still alive.
3. **Log your numbers daily** (touches / conversations / demos booked / closes). What gets measured gets fixed. 2 minutes, every day, no skipping.
4. **One demo or discovery call booked per week, minimum.** If Friday comes and the week has zero booked, Saturday is an outreach day.
5. **Protect the grind block.** 90 minutes, same time every day, phone-on-owner-mode. Being "busy" killed the last attempt — this block is the business.

Weekly non-negotiable: **Sunday review (30 min).** Look at the numbers, answer one question: what stopped closes this week? Fix that one thing next week.

## PHASE 0 — Get demo-ready (Weeks 1–2, deadline: July 24, 2026)

You can't sell what you can't show. Two weeks, then selling starts whether it feels ready or not.

- [ ] **Install ARIA on the two businesses you already have**: Acumuladores Padilla and Glamurosa Nails. Free. They're your lab rats and your first case studies.
- [ ] **Run it 2 weeks, collect real numbers**: calls answered after hours, appointments booked, messages captured. One real number ("booked 9 appointments the owner would've missed") beats any sales page.
- [ ] **Set pricing and never apologize for it**: $297/mo starter, $497/mo standard, + $500 setup fee. You can raise later; you cannot start at $99 and survive.
- [ ] **Make the funnel work end-to-end**: calculator → capture-lead → Calendly booking → Stripe payment. Test it yourself with a real card. If a stranger can't go from calculator to paid without you touching anything, fix that first.
- [ ] **Pick ONE niche for the first 25 clients.** Recommendation: appointment-based local services (nail/hair salons, barbers, dental, auto shops) — you already have proof in two of them. Niche = referrals compound.

## PHASE 1 — First 10 clients (Months 1–3, deadline: Oct 31, 2026)

Target: **10 paying clients ≈ $3–5k MRR.** This phase is won by volume of conversations, nothing else.

- Warm network first (week 1): list every business owner you know or your family knows. In a small-business community that's 30+ doors. They buy trust, not tech.
- Then local cold outreach: 10 touches/day = ~200/month → expect ~20 conversations → ~8 demos → 2–4 closes/month. Those are normal numbers; the funnel only fails if the top is empty.
- Ask every close and every "no" for one referral: "Who do you know that's losing calls?"

**The script (walk-in / call):**
> "Hi, I'm Padilla, I run a local AI company. Quick question — when someone calls you after hours or while you're with a customer, what happens? ... Right, that's usually 20–40% of calls, and most of those people just call the next shop. I built a system that answers those calls, texts back missed callers, and books them into your calendar automatically. It's running at Glamurosa Nails right now — booked [X] appointments last month that would've been lost. Can I show you a 10-minute demo this week?"

**The DM/email version:**
> "Hey [Name] — ran your numbers through my missed-call calculator: if you miss even 5 calls a week, that's roughly $[X]/year walking to competitors. I run the AI receptionist at [case study business] here in town. 10-min demo this week? Worst case you know your numbers."

## PHASE 2 — $10k MRR machine (Months 4–9, deadline: Apr 2027)

Target: **25–30 clients, $10–12k MRR.**

- Raise prices: new clients at $497–697/mo. Every 10 clients, nudge up.
- Systematize onboarding to under 2 hours of your time per client (templates, checklists, standard setup).
- Turn the 3 best client results into public case studies on padilla-ai.
- Start one repeatable inbound channel: Google Local ads pointing at the calculator, or weekly short-form content showing real client results. Pick one, run it 90 days before judging.
- Churn rule: call every client monthly. Saving a client is 5x cheaper than closing one.

## PHASE 3 — Scale to $30k, then $100k (Months 10–28)

- **$30k MRR (~late 2027):** hire first help — a VA/appointment-setter doing the outreach non-negotiables so your day is demos and closes only. Add upsells: missed-call texting + reviews + reactivation campaigns push average client to $800–1,200/mo.
- **$100k/mo (Nov 2028):** ~100–120 clients at ~$900 avg, or 60–70 clients + a high-ticket tier ($2–3k/mo full AI front-desk for multi-location businesses). Small team: 1–2 setters, 1 support/onboarding person. You are CEO, not receptionist-repairman.

## THE REAL ESTATE BRIDGE

Real estate is funded by the agency, not instead of it. The rule:
- From the first client onward, **auto-transfer 20% of every payout** to a separate "capital" account. Untouchable.
- First property conversation happens when BOTH are true: 6 months of personal expenses saved as reserves AND $15k+ MRR stable for 3 straight months (lenders want ~2 years of self-employment income anyway — that lands right around 2028).
- Likely first move: house-hack or small multi-family with agency cash flow covering the note. We'll build the specific plan when the MRR trigger hits — not before. Chasing two rabbits in year one catches zero.
